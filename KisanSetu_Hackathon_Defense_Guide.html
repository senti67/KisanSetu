2. Cost & Unit Economics
Q: What is the exact operational cost per farmer booking?
Total Unit Cost: ~₹0.45 per issued gate pass.
• IVR Voice Call: Standard cloud telephony (Exotel/Airtel) costs ₹0.35/min. Our streamlined flow completes in under 45 seconds = ₹0.28 to ₹0.30.
• Transactional DLT SMS: ₹0.12 per message.
• Edge Compute & Storage: Serverless architecture costs ₹0.0001 per request.
Funding Source: Funded by APMC Mandi Board cess (1-2% market fee) or the central Digital Agriculture Mission / PM-AASHA fund.
Q: What is the Return on Investment (ROI) for farmers and mandis?
• Fuel Savings: Eliminates 8–14 hours of tractor idling in highway queues, saving farmers ₹800 to ₹1,500 in diesel/rent per trip.
• Zero Middlemen Deductions: Protects farmers from arbitrary 3–8% "moisture penalties" and "queue cuts" imposed by middlemen.
• Post-Harvest Spoilage Prevention: Eliminates overnight parking in rain, preventing sudden grain degradation.
3. Scalability, Architecture & Peak Season Traffic
Q: How does the system handle massive spikes during the 3-week harvesting window?
• Serverless Edge Infrastructure: Nitro and TanStack Edge Workers scale horizontally on-demand to 10,000+ requests per second without cold-start bottlenecks.
• Telephony Trunking: Enterprise SIP trunking supports over 10,000 concurrent voice channels.
• Quota-Driven Load Balancing: When a mandi's 2-hour arrival window fills to capacity, the engine automatically routes farmers to neighboring sub-mandis within a 15 km radius.
Q: What happens if there is an internet failure at the Mandi gate?
• Offline Gate Verification Mode: Tokens follow a cryptographic hash format (KS-XXXX). The Gate Officer's tablet runs a PWA with local IndexedDB cache, validating token authenticity and queue positions offline, syncing automatically once connectivity is restored.
4. Usability & Rural Inclusivity
Q: How do illiterate smallholders or farmers with basic button phones use KisanSetu?
• Zero-App Feature Phone Voice Line: Farmers dial 1800-180-1551 from any ₹1,000 keypad phone. Prompts are spoken in Hindi, Punjabi, Marathi, and English.
• DTMF Keypad Input: Dialing numbers (1 for Paddy, 2 for Wheat) has a 99.9% accuracy rate, completely unaffected by rural tractor noise or heavy accents.
• Voice Read-Back + SMS: The system audibly speaks the Token ID twice at the end of the call, followed by an immediate SMS receipt.
Q: How do you prevent slot hoarding or fake bookings by middlemen?
• Mobile & Aadhaar Binding: Every booking requires 10-digit mobile verification and Aadhaar last 4 digits.
• 1-Active-Pass Limit: A single farmer identity can only hold one active, unfulfilled pass per crop season.
• Land Holding Acreage Cap: Maximum quintals bookable are capped based on registered landholding records.
5. Quality Control, MSP & Moisture Pre-Check
Q: How does the Moisture Pre-Check (17.0% threshold) work?
• Farmer Village Pre-Check: The farmer enters the reading obtained from their local digital grain moisture meter.
• Exact Boundary Rules:
  – ≤ 17.0%: Status is PASS (0% deduction) → Fast-tracks to gate pass booking.
  – > 17.0%: Status is REJECTED (Sun Drying Required) → Displays drying advice (2-4 hours on tarpaulin) before the farmer spends fuel traveling.
• Official Notice: Explicitly states that official gate laboratory testing at Gate #2 is final.
Q: Which MSP rates and payment rules are integrated?
• Hardcoded with official Government CACP 2025–26 MSP Rates: Paddy Common (₹2,300/Qtl), Paddy Grade A (₹2,320/Qtl), Wheat (₹2,425/Qtl), Mustard (₹5,950/Qtl), Chana (₹5,650/Qtl), Cotton (₹7,521/Qtl).
• Directly links DBT bank transfer expectation timeline (48–72 hours after weighbridge stamping).
